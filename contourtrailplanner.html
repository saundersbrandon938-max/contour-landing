Questions

Before you head out

Does Contour work offline in the backcountry?

Yes. Cache any route's vector map and elevation data before you leave signal — Contour keeps working with the phone in airplane mode.

Which watches can I export routes to?

GPX and FIT export are built in, with tested profiles for Garmin, Suunto, and COROS devices.

How accurate is the elevation data on exposed alpine terrain?

Contour blends satellite DEM data with corrected barometric traces from public GPX uploads, so switchback-heavy and above-treeline sections stay accurate to roughly 15 feet per mile.
